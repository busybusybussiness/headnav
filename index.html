HeadNav

Heading Numbering & Outline Sidebar for Google Docs.

HeadNav is a free Google Docs add-on that shows a clickable outline of every Heading 1-6 in your document, numbers your headings (1, 1.1, 1.2, 2...) with one click, and inserts a table of contents whose lines link to each heading.

What it does

Outline sidebar: click any heading to jump to it, and filter long outlines as you type. Number headings: add or remove hierarchical numbers; removal only strips numbers HeadNav added. Insert contents: drop a linked table of contents at your cursor.

Your data

HeadNav works only on the document you have open. It makes no outside network calls and does not collect, store, or share your document content or personal data. Read the full Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.
