Quick answers

How do I open it?

In Google Docs, choose Extensions > HeadNav > Open outline sidebar.

The sidebar is empty. HeadNav lists text styled as Heading 1 to Heading 6 (Format > Paragraph styles). Normal text that only looks big is not counted. Click Refresh after changing headings.

How do I number my headings?

Open the sidebar and click Number (or use Extensions > HeadNav > Number headings). To undo, click Unnumber (or choose Remove heading numbers). Removal only strips numbers HeadNav added and never touches text you typed yourself.

How do I insert a table of contents?

Place your cursor where you want it and click Insert contents. Each line links to its heading.

Does HeadNav see my other documents?

No. It only works on the document you have open. See the Privacy Policy page for details.
