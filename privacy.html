HeadNav Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026

HeadNav - Heading Numbering & Outline Sidebar is a Google Docs add-on published by Sleepy.

What HeadNav accesses

HeadNav only reads and edits the Google Doc you have open while you use it. It uses the "documents.currentonly" permission, which covers only the current document, and the "script.container.ui" permission, which lets it show a sidebar and menu inside Google Docs. HeadNav cannot see your Google Drive, your other documents, or your account contents.

What HeadNav does with your data

HeadNav reads the headings in your open document to show the outline sidebar, adds or removes heading numbers when you click the button, and inserts a table of contents when you ask it to. All of this happens inside Google's own Apps Script environment. HeadNav makes no outside network calls. Your document content is not sent to Sleepy, to any server we run, or to any third party.

What we collect and store

We do not collect, store, sell, or share personal data or document content. We do not use cookies or analytics inside the add-on. HeadNav stores no data outside your document.

Google user data

HeadNav's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with an updated date.
